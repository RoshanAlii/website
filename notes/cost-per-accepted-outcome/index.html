The model bill is only part of the picture. Include the work needed to turn a generated answer into an accepted result.

Illustrative worksheet

All starting values are hypothetical, not vendor prices or business results. Replace them with your own observations. Calculation inputs stay in this page and are not saved or submitted.

Define “accepted” first.

For a research brief, that might mean the required facts are present, important claims have traceable sources, and unknowns are clearly marked. Keep that definition the same when comparing approaches.

Show the calculation.

(model calls × average call cost + review hours × hourly cost + other batch costs) ÷ accepted outcomes

In the example, 200 calls cost AED 4. Five hours of review and correction cost AED 300. The AED 304 batch produces 80 accepted outcomes: AED 3.80 each. The acceptance rate is 80%.

Include costs spent on rejected work in the batch total. If nothing is accepted, cost per accepted outcome is undefined; a zero answer would be misleading.

What this comparison leaves out.

This worksheet estimates direct batch cost. Add allocated setup, storage or operating costs under “other” when relevant. Track elapsed turnaround separately: five hours of work can still sit in a queue for days.

A lower cost is useful only if the outcomes meet the same requirements. Check whether the accepted outputs are equally useful, and whether the next person can act on them.
