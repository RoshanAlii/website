Synthetic demonstration

The properties, dates and values below are invented for this exercise. This is not a market report, valuation, investment recommendation or Kirpa project.

The question

“What is the typical annual asking rent in this small sample, and what would we need before drawing a broader conclusion?” Notice that the question concerns asking rents, not signed tenancy contracts.

The claim, with its receipt

EXAMPLE FINDING

The median annual asking rent in this three-record sample is AED 90,000.

Source
Example dataset A above; synthetic, no external source.
Observation date
1 September 2026, illustrative.
Measure
Median advertised annual rent across three records.
Calculation
Sort 80,000, 90,000, 130,000. The middle observation is 90,000.
Scope
This small sample only; not a neighbourhood or market estimate.

A different measure gives a different answer.

The mean asking rent is AED 100,000: (80,000 + 90,000 + 130,000) ÷ 3. That does not contradict the median; it answers a different statistical question.

The individual annual asking rents per square foot are AED 100, AED 100 and AED 130. Their simple mean is AED 110 per square foot. Total rent divided by total area is approximately AED 111.11 per square foot. State the calculation rather than calling both “the average.”

What remains unknown

  • Whether the listings are current, duplicated or negotiable.
  • Whether the units are comparable in location, condition and lease terms.
  • What tenants actually agreed to pay.
  • Whether the sample represents a wider area or period.

Use this structure with real sources.

For each important statement, retain the original source link or dataset reference, retrieval date, field definition, filters and calculation. Where sources disagree, show the disagreement and investigate the definitions before combining them.

A real brief would need verified records and suitable comparisons before making any market claim. “Unknown” is a useful answer when the evidence is missing.
