MY APPROACH

Start with the decision.
Make the reasoning visible.

01 — Define the outcome. What does the next person need to be able to do?

02 — Understand the work. Identify the inputs, ownership and practical constraints.

03 — Check the evidence. Separate what is known from assumptions and missing information.
